Before you roll

Small habits.
Big difference.

A quick check before you ride—and a little extra awareness on the road—can help prevent common crashes and serious injuries.

01 ◉

Wear a helmet

Choose a properly fitted helmet and fasten the strap. Replace it after a crash.

Required in California for riders under 18 and all Class 3 riders
02 ✓

Check your bike

Test the brakes, check tire pressure, and confirm your lights and battery are secure.

Pause if anything feels loose or damaged
03 ↗

Be predictable

Obey signs and signals, ride with traffic, signal turns, and stay off your phone.

Look and signal before changing direction
04 ☼

Be seen and be heard

Use lights at night, wear something visible, and alert people before passing.

Slow down near people walking

California law · Corona rules

Know your
class. Know the rules.

E-bike rules depend on the bike’s class and where you ride. Check signs and local restrictions before heading onto a sidewalk, trail, or path.

CALIFORNIA

Know what you’re riding

Class 1 pedal-assist and Class 2 throttle e-bikes assist up to 20 mph; Class 3 pedal-assist e-bikes assist up to 28 mph. Riders must be at least 16 to operate a Class 3 e-bike.

California requires helmets for riders under 18 and for every Class 3 rider, regardless of age.

CORONA · ORDINANCE 3430

Take it slow on sidewalks and trails

Corona’s 2026 ordinance updates local bicycle and trail rules. On sidewalks or trails, ride at a safe speed and never faster than 10 mph. Yield to people walking and obey posted restrictions.

A local safety reminder

Speed makes
a difference.

Riverside University Health System reported that e-bike injury-related emergency visits in the county rose from 494 in 2024 to 958 in 2025. Its advice: wear a properly fitted helmet, slow down, and follow the rules of the road.
